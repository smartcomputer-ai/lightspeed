## The problem
Operations teams at mid-market companies run their approvals, handoffs and exceptions across email, spreadsheets and five or six SaaS tools. The work is repetitive, but each workflow is too small to justify an engineering project, so it stays manual.

Workflow tools exist, but they charge per seat. The value of a workflow grows with how often it runs, not with how many people log in, so per-seat pricing caps what the vendor earns and makes customers ration access.

## Why now
- AI agents can now handle the long tail of steps that used to need a person: reading an invoice, chasing a missing field, routing an exception.
- Run volume grows faster than headcount. Our customers' monthly workflow runs grew 3.4× in the last twelve months while their seats grew 1.3×.
- Mid-market buyers are consolidating tools; they want one place for workflows rather than one automation per department.

## What Lumen is
Lumen is workflow software for operations teams: build a workflow from templates, run it with people and agents in the loop, and pay for the runs as well as the seats.

**One line for the title slide:** Workflows that pay for themselves as they run.
