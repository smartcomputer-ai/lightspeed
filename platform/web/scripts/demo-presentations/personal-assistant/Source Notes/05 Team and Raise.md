# 5. Team and the raise

> Source note for the Series A narrative, §5, and deck slides 7–8. Runway is from the finance memo of Aug 20.

## Team
About 40 people. The headquarters is in Munich, with teams in Germany, the Netherlands and the UK.
- **Ada Demo:** founder and CEO.
- **Marco Ruiz:** chief of staff. He owns the board pack and the go-to-market hiring.
- **Priya Natarajan:** head of engineering, with 14 engineers in three squads.

Open roles: a Staff engineer (offer out) and Account executives, with two finalists for the first seat.

## The raise
- **€12M Series A**, with Northlight Ventures as the lead conversation.
- Runway is 19 months at the current burn.¹ After the round it is 36 months, even with the hiring plan below.

## Use of funds

| | Share | What it buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Go-to-market | 45% | 6 account executives across DE, NL and UK; partner channel |
| Product and engineering | 35% | Lumen Approvals; the platform migration (plan in September) |
| Customer success | 20% | onboarding and expansion for usage-heavy accounts |

## Milestones for the next 18 months
- ARR from €4.9M to €12M.
- Net revenue retention above 120% for every cohort at month 12.
- Lumen Approvals as the way in for a third of new customers.
