# 4. Product

> Source note for the Series A narrative, §4, and deck slide 6. The usage numbers are August's, from product analytics; September's go in when the month closes.

## The platform
- **Templates:** 140 workflow templates for finance, procurement, logistics and HR operations. Most customers start from one and change it.
- **Runs with people and agents in the loop:** every step is a person, an agent or an integration. A person approves anything that leaves the company.
- **Integrations:** 60 connectors. The ones used most are Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, SAP and HubSpot.
- **Monthly workflow runs:** 9.8M in August across 31 customers, up from 2.9M twelve months ago.

## Lumen Approvals, the second product
Launched in May 2026: approvals as a standalone product, priced per approver, that runs on the same platform. It is the entry point for teams that are not ready to build workflows yet.
- 11 of 31 customers use it; 4 came to Lumen through it.
- 2,140 weekly active approvers and 63k approvals in August.
- Median time to approve went from 2.1 days (email) to 4 hours.
