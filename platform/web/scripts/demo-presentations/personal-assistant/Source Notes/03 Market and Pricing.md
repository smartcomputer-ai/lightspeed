# 3. Market and pricing

> Source note for the Series A narrative, §3, and deck slide 5 (chart: Images/Pricing Comparison.png). The table is copied from /memory/research/competitor-pricing.md; correct it there first.

## Who we meet in deals
- **Beacon Analytics:** strongest at the low end, with a free tier for 3 users. They raised Starter from $29 to $39 in March 2026 and have not rolled it back.
- **Orbit Insights:** competes on seat price alone and has no usage component. They introduced the Scale tier in January 2026.

## List prices, per user per month

| | Lumen | Beacon Analytics | Orbit Insights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry tier | Team €49 | Starter $39 (min 5) | Growth $45 |
| Mid tier | Business €89 | Professional $79 | Scale $95 |
| Usage | €0.004 a run above 50k a month | $0.50 per 1k events above 1M a month | none |
| Annual discount | 15% | 20% | 10% |
| Free tier | 14-day trial | free for 3 users | 30-day trial |

## What it means for the deck
- Lumen's mid tier costs more per seat than Beacon's, but Lumen is the only one of the three that grows with runs. The deck's claim of "expansion from usage" stands.
- We do not try to win on seat price. The comparison is total cost at a customer's run volume, where the usage tier is cheaper than buying seats for everyone who touches a workflow.
- Neither competitor publishes net retention.

## Not known
- Orbit's enterprise floor, and whether it has any usage pricing (the site says "talk to us").
- Beacon's minimum seat count for enterprise.
